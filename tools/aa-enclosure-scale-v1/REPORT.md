retains original source-feature ordering and the original integer rectangle/disc predicates; unions
remain unions and later opaque writers mask earlier ones. The BVH rejects only boxes certainly
outside the query. Random differential tests compare indexed and exhaustive root color ranges and
independent raw-chain membership, not a primitive list generated by the producer.

Every pixel has a fixed100000-cell and75/64-second share. Unused shares are not transferred. Pixel
state, precision, cells/depth, unresolved area and CPU/wall are retained. A one-cell-quota control
retains all64 initial enclosures, demonstrating no early-pixel cell-budget starvation. Completed
pixel records are atomically checkpointed; hard process limits preserve these checkpoints, while
uncompleted pixels remain unknown. No hard termination was needed in the measured run.

The constructor rejects nonfinite/out-of-domain coordinates (±1e6), unsupported dyadic denominators
above2^128, and bounded feature/chain/vertex/color violations before primitive construction. All32
frozen geometry files are supported. Exact dyadic input assumptions and rational accumulation are
unchanged. No floating epsilon or libm is used in the enclosure predicates.

## Alpha and quantization ambiguity

Two bounded isolated union-coverage jobs use the original isolated dateline/anchor feature tiles,
painted white over black solely to enclose coverage. Both attain1/10240. Each has2 ambiguous alpha
codes among64 pixels, or2 among16 potentially partial pixels. Combined4/128 =3.125%, or4/32 =12.5%
of potentially partial pixels. These correlated, structured cases do not establish a population
ambiguity probability. Both have zero RGB565-quantization-ambiguous pixels: alpha and RGB565 rounding
are genuinely distinct diagnostics. Full sequential blending is not implemented.

Primary coarse jobs have35 ambiguous RGB565 pixels (36 channels) out of2560 pixels; the eight fine
repeats have2 ambiguous pixels/channels out of512. Repeated tiles and the precision-limited job are
retained in these descriptive counts; they are not independent-population ambiguity estimates.

Any ambiguous channel requires discrete possible codes; ordinary continuous-error propagation
cannot bridge its rounding step. Shared alpha couples channels, and per-channel Cartesian ranges
are conservative supersets. The same warning applies to denominator/floor handling in a future
quality comparison. No reference-dependent support mask is used for a scoring claim here.

## Best defensible full-fixture estimate (not a measurement)

The preregistered model interpolates tile CPU piecewise-linearly in density between the five sampled
anchors for each measured condition. It applies these curves to every condition's geometry-only
density distribution. For unsampled fixtures use the median style-specific curve; cross-fixture
min/max curves form **scenario ranges, not confidence intervals**. Fine costs use completed primary
matched ratios. The censored eighth pair is retained and makes fine completion uncertain.

Assuming a retained geometry/BVH index across tiles, the model gives:
- Coarse1/2560: about5.06CPU-hours for32 conditions; scenario3.90–6.45h.
- Fine1/10240: illustrative20.98CPU-hours; completed-case scenario13.66–27.75h.
- Per fixture central models: coarse0.038–0.303h; fine0.157–1.256h.
- Rebuilding/parsing in a new process per tile instead gives a coarse central projection13.59h.

These are conditional cost models, NOT measured full-frame performance or bounds on convergence.
Sixteen population tiles exceed the density support of the measured style curves; the model holds
the end cost there and may understate those tails. Density does not encode edge orientation, union
complexity, background contrast or opaque occlusion. There is one sample at each specified density
anchor, so no reliable sampling CI can be justified. The pre-execution phrase “censored projections
are lower bounds” must be read narrowly: a censored JOB's spent time is a lower bound on its own
completion work; the GLOBAL model is neither a rigorous lower nor upper bound. The fine scenario's
upper endpoint27.75h is not a completion guarantee. Sidecar/layer passes, sequential outcomes and
certification bookkeeping are excluded; no arbitrary “double it” multiplier is asserted.
