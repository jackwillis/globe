COASTLINES, BORDERS, COUNTRIES, LAKES
Made with Natural Earth, 1:110m coastline, boundary lines and Admin 0
Countries, and 1:50m lakes (used only to mark water in the elevation grid).
Public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Natural Earth draws de facto boundaries. Borders and country shapes are
cartographic references, not statements about sovereignty or status.

ELEVATION AND SEA FLOOR
NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second
Global Relief Model (60 arc-second ice-surface grid), DOI 10.25921/fd45-gt74.
Public domain / CC0 1.0. Resampled to coarse grids (one degree in the Mac OS 9 build).

CITIES AND PLACE NAMES
Wikidata structured data (entity labels, identifiers, coordinates and
population): CC0 1.0. https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
No Wikipedia article text is included. Names are shown in plain ASCII
(accents removed). The list is not a complete or authoritative gazetteer.
