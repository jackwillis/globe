# Data and third-party licensing

Adopted by the owner on 2026-11-13.

| Decision | Licenses |
| --- | --- |
| Allowed for data | Public domain, CC0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Hold for explicit owner review and an ADR or issue before use | ODbL (including OpenStreetMap), any NC or ND license, CC BY-SA 3.0 and earlier, CC BY 3.0 and earlier, and any unreviewed or unstated license |

Record each source in [data/SOURCES.md](../data/SOURCES.md) with its name, URL, license, version or date, what was adapted, and attribution text.

Existing sources include Natural Earth (public domain), NOAA ETOPO 2022 (public domain/CC0), Wikidata (CC0), and adapted RESOLVE Ecoregions (CC BY 4.0).

Open questions include use of OpenStreetMap under ODbL and the public-domain status of US Census TIGER data.

Third-party code is reviewed separately. Obtain review before incorporating it.
