# UI audit after sidebar integration (#90), 2026-11-09

Status: proposal for owner approval. Nothing below has been removed except where marked *done*.
Evidence is from the code and host tests; nothing here was observed on the G4 or on a real X display.

## Space budget at 640x480

Both front ends now give the sidebar 220 px. On a 640-wide screen that is 34 percent of the width,
leaving a 420 px globe pane (OS 9 sizes its window to the screen; X11 now defaults to a 420x440
globe, 640x440 window — *done*, it was 640x480 plus the sidebar, i.e. 860 px wide, a regression of
the single-window change). Recommendation: keep 220 px as the maximum; consider 180 px if the
information card is the only thing that needs the width (wrapped text already copes).

## Copying information (#87)

A. **Copy Information**: Edit > Copy (Cmd-C) copies the whole current card as text.
 OS 9: `ZeroScrap()` then `PutScrap(len, 'TEXT', ptr)` with the card's lines joined by CR; about 30 lines,
 no new allocation beyond a stack buffer of a few hundred bytes (cards have at most 12 lines of at most
 about 64 characters). Works with the existing hand-drawn sidebar and with wrapping, and Copy is
 enabled whenever a card is shown. X11: answer `SelectionRequest` for `CLIPBOARD`/`PRIMARY`
 after Ctrl-C (about 60 lines). Text is ASCII, so no encoding translation until #84.
B. **TextEdit record**: native selection and scrap, but a new TERec (heap, tens of KB with styles off),
 manual scrolling with a scroll bar, activation and focus rules with the other windows, its own
 wrapping (which would replace wrap.hpp), localization refresh on language change, and still-custom
 X11 work. An order of magnitude more code and risk.

Recommendation: do A first. Full selection is not needed for v0.1. Not started; awaiting owner go-ahead.

## Scale (#81)

The projection is orthographic with `Camera::radius_px` the projected globe radius, so at the view
centre one pixel is exactly `kEarthMeanRadiusKm / radius_px` km in every direction. Away from the
centre it is foreshortened radially by cos(angle from centre) and unchanged tangentially, so a
fixed bar elsewhere is misleading. Recommendation: label it "scale at center", compute a 1-2-5
length from `radius_px`, recalculate on zoom and resize (north orientation does not matter at the
centre). Without an on-map text renderer (#36), show it in the sidebar (a line of text plus a bar
under the information card) rather than over the globe: about 80 lines of core (nice-length
selection, units-aware per #85) and 40 per front end. Over-the-globe drawing waits on #36.
Compass rose: defer. A small north arrow (the projected direction of the pole, with a hidden
state when the pole is on the near side) is a possible later step; not designed yet.
