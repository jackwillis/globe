Poodle Earth

A free, offline globe for PowerPC Macs running Mac OS 9.

Download Poodle Earth View on GitHub

Version 0.1.0 public preview · StuffIt (.sit)

Mac OS 9 screenshots coming soon

Explore the globe

  • Find a place. Drag to turn the globe, double-click to zoom, and click a country or city for its information card.
  • Change the view. Use Map for biome colors or Relief for elevation. Press D to shade the night side.
  • Measure a distance. Press M and pick two points to see the great-circle distance.

Get started on Mac OS 9

  1. Open the .sit file with StuffIt Expander.
  2. Copy the folder to your hard disk and open Poodle Earth.

Read the Classic ReadMe for v0.1.0 for the full controls and troubleshooting.

Compatibility and known limits

Tested on a 467 MHz Power Mac G4 with 256 MB of RAM running Mac OS 9.2.2. Other Mac models and system versions are untested. The app’s memory partition defaults to 24 MB preferred and 12 MB minimum.

The compact atlas omits many small places. If the globe’s edge draws incompletely, turn it slightly or zoom to repaint it.
