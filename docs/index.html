Poodle Earth

An interactive globe for your classic Macintosh. Spin the Earth, explore countries and cities, and see the shape of the land. The whole atlas works offline.

Download Poodle Earth View on GitHub

Version 0.1.0 public preview · StuffIt (.sit) · PowerPC Mac OS 9

Explore the globe

Map and Relief
Switch from a map colored by biome to a view shaded by elevation.
Countries and cities
Click a country or city to open its information card.
Distance and daylight
Measure the great-circle distance between two points or shade the night side of the Earth.

The preview uses a compact world atlas, so many smaller places are missing. If the edge of the globe draws incompletely, turn it a little or zoom to repaint it.

Get started on Mac OS 9

The download is for PowerPC Macs running Mac OS 9. The tested setup is a Power Mac G4 (AGP, 467 MHz, 256 MB RAM) running Mac OS 9.2.2. Other Mac models and system versions are untested.

  1. Expand the downloaded .sit archive with StuffIt Expander on your Mac.
  2. Copy the expanded folder to your hard disk and double-click Poodle Earth.
  3. Drag to turn the globe. Double-click a spot to center and zoom in.

See the Classic ReadMe for v0.1.0 for controls, memory settings, and troubleshooting.
